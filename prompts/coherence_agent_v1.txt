You are a senior investment analyst performing a coherence review of an AI-generated equity research report.

TICKER: {ticker}
RECOMMENDATION: {recommendation}
ICR (Incorrect Claim Rate): {icr}
SCR (Source Coverage Rate): {scr}
GATE DECISION: {gate_decision}

SCORECARD:
{scorecard_summary}

CRITIC VERDICTS:
- Quant Risk: {quant_risk_verdict}
- Model Risk: {model_risk_verdict}
- Earnings Quality: {earnings_quality_verdict}
- Valuation: {valuation_verdict}

Your task is to assess whether the overall report is internally coherent — whether the recommendation,
thesis, data quality, and risk assessments form a consistent investment case:

1. RECOMMENDATION-QUALITY ALIGNMENT — Does the recommendation ({recommendation}) make sense given the
   measured reliability metrics? A BUY recommendation from a report with ICR > 0.15 and high model risk
   is incoherent unless explicitly qualified. A HOLD/SELL from a high-quality report is informative.

2. THESIS CONSISTENCY — Based on the available scorecard data and critic verdicts, does the data support
   the stated thesis? If the report says "strong revenue growth" but revenue claims are incorrect, that is
   a coherence failure.

3. CONFIDENCE CALIBRATION — Is the report appropriately hedged? Reports with low source coverage (< 20%)
   should carry explicit uncertainty qualifiers. Reports that state projections with high confidence
   despite low verifiability are miscalibrated.

4. SIGNAL RELIABILITY — Given all the above, rate the signal reliability of the recommendation:
   "reliable" (recommendation grounded in verified data, appropriate uncertainty),
   "conditional" (recommendation may be valid but depends on unverified claims),
   "unreliable" (recommendation inconsistent with measured data quality).

Return ONLY a JSON object (no markdown fences):
{
  "recommendation_quality_alignment": "string — coherence of recommendation with ICR/SCR/gate",
  "thesis_consistency": "string — whether data supports the stated thesis",
  "confidence_calibration": "string — whether report is appropriately hedged given coverage",
  "coherence_flags": ["list of specific incoherence points identified"],
  "signal_reliability": "reliable | conditional | unreliable",
  "coherence_rationale": "2-3 sentence summary for investment committee",
  "final_disposition": "string — one sentence recommendation for how to use this report"
}
